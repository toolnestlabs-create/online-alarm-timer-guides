# Online Alarm and Timer Guides

Simple notes on using an online alarm clock, countdown timer and stopwatch in your browser. No download is needed. Keep the tab open and your sound on so the alert can play.

Common uses: a 5 minute break timer, a 15 minute study sprint, a 30 minute workout timer, or a wake-up alarm.

Free alarm clock and timers: https://alarmsclock.live/
